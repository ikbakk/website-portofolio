## Findings

### 1. HIGH — The sections lab still reads like unpublished preparation notes

- **Location:** `src/pages/sections-lab.astro`, especially the “Data selection” section.
- **Rule:** R-38, real content or an honestly labeled placeholder.
- **Evidence:** The page says its source is “working source material to verify before publishing” and lists “a final contact route” as still needed. The home page already provides the portfolio email, GitHub, and LinkedIn links. The lab is a built, user-facing route, so this wording makes the published portfolio sound unfinished and includes a stale requirement.
- **Suggested direction:** Remove or update the preparation notes using only the currently published project and contact details.

### 2. LOW — The home page opening and contact prompt rely on broad, familiar phrases

- **Location:** `src/pages/index.astro`, hero and contact sections.
- **Rules:** R-15, specific calls to action; R-16, specific copy over generic claims.
- **Evidence:** “I build for real life,” “websites, apps, and tools for the people who use them,” “Explore selected work,” and “Let’s build something useful” could describe many engineering portfolios. The project cards below them contain more specific material that could give these lines a clearer voice and make the work link more direct.
- **Suggested direction:** Ground the opening and contact invitation in the kinds of projects already described, and name the action the work link performs.
